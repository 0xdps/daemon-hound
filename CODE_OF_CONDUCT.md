# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

**Examples of positive behavior:**

- Constructive, specific feedback
- Respectful disagreement
- Helping others learn and grow
- Staying on topic

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to moderate, remove, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this code of conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private discussion with the maintainers.
